# Design identity — Archive Lifeboat

The application is a horizontal conservation bench. Four translucent film strips lie across a warm daylight surface and can be scrubbed left-to-right. A movable loupe enlarges the active strip. Inspection clamps the strips; sealing embosses the final record. Controls live in a slim vertical tool rail, never in a bottom bar or dashboard grid.

Signature interaction: scrub the film until registration marks align, inspect the fidelity matrix through the loupe, then pull a physical-looking seal lever.
